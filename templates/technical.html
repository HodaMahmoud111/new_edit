DESIGN DECISIONS AND WHY
One reference

No external unverified source is introduced into answers, strictly preserving PFP-guideline clinical consistency and eliminating out-of-domain hallucinations.

Top-3, not one result

Offers multiple supporting passages across document sections without risking context loss or over-constraining the model to a single segment.

Separated model roles

LLM is dedicated strictly to synthesis and clinical formatting; deterministic dense embeddings and cosine similarity handle objective retrieval and validation.

Failure is recorded

Generation refusal or low semantic similarity is treated as a separate operational safeguard rather than guessing clinical recommendations.
